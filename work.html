Roblox Strategy Tower Defense

Spirit Feast Battle

In early 2024, I created a game project called Spirit Feast Battle. This game is a tower defense game with a unique theme based on Indonesia's gerobak (food cart) culinary culture. Each tower in the game represents a different gerobak, and has its own special abilities inspired by the kind of food it sells.

▼
2024 Roblox Studio Smart AI Detection

I worked on this game completely by myself and finished it in just 5 months. It includes many features that make the gameplay more interesting and strategic. Each tower can attack enemies in different ways, such as using explosions, speeding up attacks, throwing boomerangs, and more. Not only the towers, but the enemies in this game also have special abilities. Some enemies can teleport, some can steal resources, and some become faster when angry. Because of this, players need to think carefully and change their strategies as the game goes on.

Roblox Obby AI

Zombie Chase

My first ever game I created on Roblox Studio. This game has a simple genre (Parkour / Obby) that uses an AI implementation for a feature that hasn’t been on other games before. The main goal for this game is to get to the finish line while you need to go through all the obstacles while being chased by the zombies.

▼
2021 Pathfinding AI Behavior Multiplayer

One of the game’s main features is that the parkour course changes every round. This means that each time a player starts a new game, the obstacle layout will be different. This makes the game more challenging and keeps the gameplay fresh, so players cannot just memorize one path, they have to stay focused and adapt to every new round. The game also provides over 5 different maps, each with its own look and feel. These maps offer different environments, giving players a variety of experiences and increasing the game’s replay value.

04
Roblox Obby Adventure

Mount Putri

I decided to follow a popular trend on Roblox: mountain climbing games. I created a new game called Mount Putri. While many players were looking for mountain-themed games, I wanted to make sure my version felt different and more exciting than the ones already on the charts.

▼
2025 Exploration Obstacles Parkour

The most unique feature of Mount Putri is the transportation system. Instead of just walking to the top, players can ride a cart that travels all the way around the mountain. I worked hard to design a track that gives players a beautiful panoramic view of the entire landscape from different angles. This makes the gameplay much more immersive and relaxing, as players can enjoy the scenery while they move through the map. I also spent time making the cart movement smooth so that the experience feels high-quality and fun for everyone who joins. I also added special obstacles that you won’t find in other mountain games. This project helped me improve my skills in environment design and vehicle scripting. Even though I followed a popular trend, Mount Putri shows that I can take a common idea and add my own creative twist to make it stand out for the players.

Roblox Luau Simulation

Roadshop Keeper

A horror experience where players take on the role of a young character who is left in charge of their uncle’s roadside shop while he is away on a trip to Jakarta. Strange things happens and players must navigate a series of chilling events.

▼
2025 Multiple Choice Interactive Smart NPC

This project was developed by a team of four: two 3D modelers/texturers, one game tester, and myself as the programmer and game designer. I was responsible for the core game mechanics, system implementation, UI design, and narrative design, bringing the overall vision to life through interactive gameplay. The game features a fully implemented enemy AI system and also includes story objectives that guide players through key plot points, an item interaction system that allows players to pick up and use objects in their environment, also a decision-making mechanic that forces players to choose between critical options (e.g., pick A or B) that can alter the course of the story.

Roblox Luau Obby

Ultimate Troll Tower

A challenging troll obby tower with unpredictable traps, secret shortcuts, and competitive leaderboards.

▼
2025 Trap Systems Leaderboard Level Design

Ultimate Troll Tower is a high-difficulty obby experience with hand-crafted troll mechanics designed to surprise and challenge players. I engineered a modular trap system where each obstacle has configurable timing, trigger conditions, and reset behavior — making it easy to design new floors without rewriting logic. The game includes a global leaderboard tracking fastest completions, a checkpoint system with anti-cheat validation, and secret paths rewarding exploration.
